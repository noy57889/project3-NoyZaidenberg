STEP BY STEP

The Sunday asado ritual

1. El Fuego (Lighting the Fire)

Quebracho wood and charcoal burn down until glowing red brasas are ready.

11:30 AM

2. La Picada & Achuras

Chorizo, morcilla, and provoleta hit the grill first with bread and chimichurri.

12:30 PM

3. Los Cortes Principales

Asado de tira and vacío cook slowly over steady heat with coarse salt only.

01:30 PM

4. Un Aplauso para el Asador

Everyone gathers at the table to applaud the person who tended the fire.

02:15 PM

THE TABLE

Simple ingredients, deep flavor.

Good Argentine beef does not need heavy marinades. Fire, coarse salt, and good company do all the work.

Brasas, never flames

Meat cooks over indirect glowing embers so the outside stays crisp and the inside juicy.

Chimichurri & Salsa Criolla

Parsley, garlic, oregano, vinegar, and olive oil served right at the table.

Malbec pairing

A glass of Mendoza Malbec balances the richness of every cut.
